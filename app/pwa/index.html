What this is

A research tool, not advice

Every figure is computed from public market data. The gates reject companies on measurable grounds and show the number behind each rejection. Nothing here is a recommendation, and no position sizing is implied.

Why bands, not ranks

The score failed its backtest

Over three annual rebalances the top decile returned 42.0% against a universe mean of 31.3% — but the bottom decile returned 49.7% and rank correlation was −0.078. The relationship is U-shaped, so the score groups companies rather than ordering them.

Known gaps

What is not modelled

Order books and capacity, which live only in filings prose. Segment mix, so a company with 5% theme revenue scores like a pure play. Three of twelve gates lack data and report unknown, which is never treated as a pass.
